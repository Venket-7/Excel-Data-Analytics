# Excel Data Analytics Portfolio

A collection of **Microsoft Excel data analytics projects** focused on retail sales, profitability, product performance, outlet performance, customer segments, and business insights.

These projects demonstrate practical skills in **data cleaning, PivotTables, PivotCharts, slicers, KPI development, data analysis, dashboard design, and business insight generation**.

---

## 📂 Projects

### 1. BlinkIT Grocery Retail Analytics

An Excel-based grocery retail analysis project focused on understanding sales performance across **product categories, outlet types, outlet sizes, location tiers, fat content, and outlet establishment years**.

#### Key Analysis

- Sales by Item Type
- Sales by Outlet Type
- Sales by Outlet Size
- Sales by Location Tier
- Low Fat vs Regular Product Sales
- Sales by Outlet Establishment Year
- Average Sales per Record
- Product and Outlet Performance

#### KPIs

- **Total Sales:** ₹1.2M
- **Average Sales per Record:** ₹141
- **Total Item Records:** 8,523
- **Unique Products:** 1,559
- **Total Outlets:** 10
- **Average Customer Rating:** 3.97 / 5

#### Tools & Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning
- Data Standardization
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Slicers
- KPI Development
- Comparative Analysis
- Dashboard Design
- Business Insight Generation

📁 **Project Folder:** `01-BlinkIT-Grocery-Retail-Analytics`
